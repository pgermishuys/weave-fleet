    An inset row that says what's happening
    Row
    
        
    Today's rows are 40px full-width strips with a 12px title and a 3px bar when active. Proposed: a 32px rounded row inset from the edge, a 13px title, and the state in words on the right: "Needs input", "Working" or when it was last active.
    
        40px strip · 3px bar · 12px → 32px row · r8 · 13px + status

    One sheet instead of four boxes
    Tokens
    
        
    Today every panel is a square box with a solid border and an 8px gap, in four near-identical greys. Proposed: rail and list sit on the window colour; conversation and right panel share one raised sheet with 12px corners and translucent borders.
    
        radius 0 · #0a0a0b #0c0c0d #111113 #141416 → r8 / r12 · chrome + sheet + raised

    Two text sizes, not twenty-eight
    Tokens
    
        
    Components use 28 font sizes today, mostly 10–11px. Proposed: 13px for the UI, 14px for reading, 12px for metadata. Use the Font toggle to compare Inter with your system font on this machine.
    
        9 · 10 · 11 · 12 · 13 · 15px… → 12 / 13 / 14px

    Motion that shows what changed
    Motion
    
        
    Press Play activity in both versions. Today rows pop in and out in one frame, and collapsing a project waits on a 1000px max-height transition. Proposed: rows open and close at their real height, and neighbours slide.
    
        0.25s ease · max-height 1000px → 150ms ease-out · measured height

    Step 2 · Push

        poll 15s + spinner → cached + pushed over SignalR
