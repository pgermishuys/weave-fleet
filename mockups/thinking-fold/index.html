Thinking

Settings → Appearance. How the model's thinking shows in conversations. Applies to every session and harness; nothing is deleted.

Today

  • Every thinking block is printed in full and stays open, so a long session is mostly thinking.
  • Nothing folds it and no setting hides it.
  • It does help to watch while a turn is running.

A · Folded to one line recommended

  • Each thinking block becomes one line: Thinking and its gist (the summary when the harness sends one, otherwise the first sentence).
  • While it streams, the line shows the newest sentence, so you can still follow along, and nothing jumps when it ends.
  • Try: click a line to open that block. It stays open until you close it.
  • Settings → Appearance → Thinking: Folded (default) · Open (today) · Hidden.
  • Like Claude's and ChatGPT's “Thought for…” lines.

B · Show thinking switch

  • A switch in the conversation header shows or hides every thinking block.
  • One click, and the setting is remembered.
  • All or nothing: to read one block you show them all again.
  • The header gains a control that's there for every turn.
  • Try: flip the switch, then replay.

Either way

  • It's only how the thinking shows. The harness and the history keep all of it.
  • The phone app doesn't show thinking today, so it's unchanged.
  • Out of scope: how long the model thought. Fleet doesn't get that time from every harness yet.
